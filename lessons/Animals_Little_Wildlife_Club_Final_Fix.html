04

Can it do that?

Grammar · 8 min
CAN / CAN'T

Use can + verb to talk about ability.
Use can't + verb when something is not possible.

✓ CAN
A bird can fly.
A dolphin can swim.
A kangaroo can jump.
✕ CAN'T
A fish can't walk.
A penguin can't fly.
A snail can't run fast.

Remember: after can / can't, use the verb without to and without -s.

★

Wildlife travel briefing

Homework · 20–25 min
YOUR ROLE: You are planning a nature trip.

Imagine a friend is visiting a place famous for its wildlife. Prepare a short, useful wildlife briefing to help them understand one animal they may see there.

Choose a real destination: a national park, a forest, a coastline, an island or a wildlife reserve. Choose one animal that lives there.

YOUR FIELD BRIEFING
  1. Destination: Where can visitors go?
  2. Animal: What animal might they see?
  3. Habitat: Where does it live?
  4. Abilities: What can it do? Give two examples.
  5. Limitations: What can't it do?
  6. Behaviour: Describe how it moves using fast, slowly or well.
THE LANGUAGE CHALLENGE

Write a short guide of 6–8 sentences. Use can at least twice, can't at least once, and one adverb. Keep the sentences clear and informative — imagine a real visitor will use your guide.

🎙️ ORAL BRIEFING

Record a 45–60 second audio guide for your visitor. Introduce the destination and animal, describe its abilities and give one useful tip, for example: “You can see…” or “Please don't…”

BE READY TO DISCUSS

At the next lesson, give your partner three clues about the animal without naming it. Your partner must guess the animal and ask you one question about its habitat or abilities.

SELF-CHECK
  • ☐ My destination and animal are real.

  • ☐ I used can, can't and an adverb correctly.
  • ☐ My information is useful to a visitor.
  • ☐ I recorded a 45–60 second audio briefing.

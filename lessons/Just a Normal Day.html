Breakfast in Porto, lunch in Madrid

I work in Madrid.

Flats in Madrid are very expensive. In Porto, flats are cheap and the beaches are beautiful, but my job is in Madrid. So I live in Porto and I work in Madrid!

I fly from Porto to Madrid on Monday morning. I work there for four days and I stay in a small flat with my brother. Then I fly home on Thursday night. On Fridays I work at home.

I don't speak Spanish very well, so I study Spanish at a language school on Saturdays. I love Porto. It's an old, beautiful city and it's great to live here!

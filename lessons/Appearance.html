03 · READING A

Grey is great!

12–14 min · individual → pairs

Before reading, look at portrait C. What words would you use to describe her hair? Do you think people always try to hide grey hair? Read the text to find out.

Grey is great! Why more people are embracing their natural hair

For many years, some people felt that grey hair made them look older, so they regularly coloured it. Today, attitudes are changing. More women and men are choosing to keep their natural silver or grey hair. For some, it is simply easier: they do not need to visit the hairdresser every few weeks. For others, it is a way to express confidence and individuality.

The change became more noticeable during the Covid-19 pandemic. When hair salons closed, many people could not colour their hair as usual. Some saw their natural colour for the first time in years and decided they liked it. Fashion magazines and celebrities also helped make silver-grey hair more visible and accepted.

Of course, there is no single right choice. Some people enjoy experimenting with colour, while others prefer their natural look. The important thing is to feel comfortable with your own decision rather than follow pressure from other people.

Reading task 1 · Gist

Choose the best summary.

The main idea is that…

Reading task 2 · Read for detail

1. Why did some people colour their hair regularly?
2. What happened during the pandemic?
3. Why did some people keep their natural colour?

Reading task 3 · Vocabulary in context

Find words in the text that mean:

  1. the colour that your hair naturally has;
  2. the quality of believing in yourself;
  3. the quality of being different from other people.
Suggested answers

1. natural colour · 2. confidence · 3. individuality

Discuss: Is grey hair viewed differently for men and women in your culture? Why might that be? Do you think fashion and social media influence these attitudes?
04 · READING B

Short is super!

12–14 min · individual → pairs

Look at portrait D. Is it possible to make assumptions about someone's abilities based on their height? Read and check the writer's message.

Short is super! Does height really matter?

People often connect height with success. In basketball, for example, being tall can be an advantage because players need to reach high and block shots. Long legs may also help in some sports. However, height is not the only thing that matters.

In sports such as gymnastics and martial arts, a shorter person may have advantages. A lower centre of gravity can help with balance, and quick reactions can be very useful. In some activities, being shorter may make certain movements easier. Skills, practice and determination are important too.

There are also studies suggesting that height is not a simple measure of health or ability. Some research has found differences in health and life expectancy between groups, but many factors affect these results, including lifestyle, environment and access to healthcare. It would be wrong to say that one height is always better than another.

The message is simple: height is one physical characteristic, not a measure of someone's value. Instead of judging people by their appearance, we should pay attention to their skills, interests and personality.

Reading task 1 · Gist

What is the writer's main point?

Reading task 2 · True or false?

1. Height can be an advantage in basketball.
2. The writer says height is the only factor in sports performance.
3. Balance and quick reactions may help in some sports.
4. The text says one height is always healthier.

Reading task 3 · Find the evidence

Complete the sentences with information from the text.

  1. Being tall can help in basketball because…
  2. A shorter person may have an advantage in some sports because…
  3. The writer says we should pay attention to people's…
Suggested answers

1. players need to reach high and block shots. 2. a lower centre of gravity can help with balance and quick reactions can be useful. 3. skills, interests and personality.

Think critically: Which statements in the text are examples, and which are the writer's opinions? Do you agree with the final paragraph?

05 · LANGUAGE FOCUS

What does she look like? What is she like?

10–12 min

LOOK + adjective

Describe appearance or impression.

She looks elegant.
He looks tired.

LOOK LIKE + noun

Say who or what someone resembles.

She looks like her mother.
He looks like an actor.

BE LIKE

Ask about / describe personality.

What's she like?
She's friendly and confident.
Notice the question forms:
What does he look like? → physical appearance
What is he like? → personality / general character
Who does he look like? → similarity

Practice A · Choose the correct option

1. What ___ your new colleague look like?
2. She ___ her mother — they have the same eyes.
3. You ___ happy today.
4. What's your new teacher ___?
5. He ___ a famous singer.

Practice B · Correct the mistakes

  1. What is your sister look like?
  2. He look like his father.
  3. What's your new neighbour look like? (You want to ask about personality.)
Answer key

1. What does your sister look like? 2. He looks like his father. 3. What's your new neighbour like?

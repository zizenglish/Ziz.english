05 / GRAMMAR STUDIO · 10 MIN

Present Simple,
in real life.

Use it for routines, repeated actions, facts and situations that are generally true.

The structure

Build the sentence.

FormStructureExample
AffirmativeSubject + base verbI work remotely.
He / She / Itverb + -s / -esShe manages a team.
Negativedon't / doesn't + base verbHe doesn't drive.
QuestionDo / Does + subject + base verb?Does she work nights?
Remember: After does / doesn't, use the base form: Does he work? not Does he works?
Spelling notes

Third-person singular

Most verbs add -s: work → works. Add -es after -sh, -ch, -ss, -x, -o: watch → watches. Consonant + y changes to -ies: study → studies. Have → has.

Adverbs of frequency
always · usually · often · sometimes · rarely · never
They usually go before the main verb: She often works late. But after be: He is always busy.

Interview your partner. Ask follow-up questions and react naturally — don't just read the prompts.

Interview cards

Ask & explore.

Two-part homework: read about people with more than one job, then produce a short profile of a working life.

Homework · Part A / Reading

Weekend work

From Monday to Friday, Amira Khan works as a doctor at a city hospital. Her weekdays are demanding: she sees patients, writes reports and works with a medical team. She says the job is rewarding, but she rarely has much free time.

At weekends, Amira sings with a local jazz band. The band plays in small venues around the city. “Music gives me a different kind of energy,” she says. “I don't earn much from it, but I love performing.”

Leo Martin has two jobs, too. During the week, he works as a taxi driver. On Saturdays, he helps his brother in a bakery. He enjoys meeting customers, but he doesn't get many full days off.

Part B / Present Simple in context

Complete the profile

Use the Present Simple form of the verbs in brackets.

My brother (work) for a local newspaper. He (not / see) his friends very often because he (have) a busy schedule. He usually (get up) early and (write) articles in the morning. (do) he enjoy his job? Yes, he (do), but he sometimes (wish) he had more free time.

05 / MISSION

Health rules that make sense

Let's look at simple facts and results. This is where the Zero Conditional comes in.

Grammar focus

Zero Conditional

We use it for facts, general truths and things that usually happen.

If + Present Simple→Present Simple
If you don't sleep enough, you feel tired.
If people drink water, they feel better.
If I eat too much, I feel sick.
Notice the pattern

Cause → result

If you feel ill, you stay at home.
If she has a headache, she takes a painkiller.
If he doesn't drink water, he gets thirsty.

Remember

Two important details

1. Use the Present Simple in both parts.
2. With he / she / it, remember -s: feels, takes, gets.

The if-clause can come first or second: If I feel ill, I stay home. / I stay home if I feel ill.

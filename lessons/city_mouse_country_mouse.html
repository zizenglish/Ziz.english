5

Read the story — in short

Read the adapted summary. Then put the events in order.

1. Two different homes. The City Mouse lives in a big city with shops, restaurants and tall buildings. The Country Mouse lives in a small, cozy nest under a tree.

2. An invitation. The City Mouse visits his friend and talks about theatres, museums and fancy food. The Country Mouse becomes curious about city life.

3. A big surprise. The Country Mouse visits the city. The house looks enormous, but the mice live in a dark basement with many other mice.

4. Dinner trouble. There is a wonderful feast: cheese, bread, cakes and Chinese food. Suddenly, a cat jumps onto the table and chases the mice. They hide in a hole in the wall.

5. The decision. When the cat leaves, the food is gone. The Country Mouse wants to go home. She prefers her quiet country life.

Grammar snapshot

Present simple · likes & routines

He likes the city. / She lives in the country.

He doesn't like the basement. / She doesn't want to stay.

Do you prefer the city? — Yes, I do. / No, I don't.

Make the sentences true for you:

  1. I live in / near ...
  2. I like ... because ...
  3. I don't like ... because ...
  4. I prefer ...

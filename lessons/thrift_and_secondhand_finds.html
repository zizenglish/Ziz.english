Smart Shopping & Upcycling Vocabulary

1. bargain
/ˈbɑː.ɡɪn/ • noun
Something bought for much less than its usual price; a great deal.
“This €5 wool coat was the bargain of the year.”
2. thrift store / charity shop
/ˈθrɪft stɔː/ • noun
A shop selling donated or second-hand goods at low prices.
“We always check the local charity shop on Saturdays.”
3. brand-new
/ˌbrændˈnjuː/ • adjective
Completely new and never worn or used before.
“The tags are still attached — it’s brand-new!”
4. pre-loved
/ˌpriːˈlʌvd/ • adjective
Previously owned and cared for by someone else (second-hand).
“My bookshelf is full of pre-loved art books.”
5. vintage
/ˈvɪn.tɪdʒ/ • adj./noun
From an earlier decade (20+ years old) and valued for classic quality.
“She collects vintage 1980s silk scarves.”
6. receipt
/rɪˈsiːt/ • noun
Paper or digital proof showing what you bought and paid.
“Keep your receipt in case you want a refund.”
7. fit
/fɪt/ • verb
To be the right size and shape for a person or space.
“The boots were cheap, but they didn’t fit me.”
8. upcycle
/ˈʌpˌsaɪ.kəl/ • verb
To creatively transform an old item into something fresher or more useful.
“He upcycled old wooden crates into plant stands.”
9. flawed
/flɔːd/ • adjective
Having a small mark, scratch, or imperfection; not 100% perfect.
“The vase is slightly flawed, so it’s half price.”
10. rummage
/ˈrʌm.ɪdʒ/ • verb
To search enthusiastically through a pile or box of things to find a gem.
“We love to rummage through boxes of old postcards.”

Three Treasures from Weekend Markets

Every Saturday morning, three friends from our art collective — Maya, Leo, and Clara — arrive at the Old Mill Flea Market at 8:30 AM, before the aisles become too crowded to move. Instead of buying brand-new items at chain stores, they love to rummage through stalls for pre-loved pieces with personality.

The power cord was too short to reach her wall socket easily, and the metal shade was slightly flawed with two tiny dents, yet the switch still clicked smoothly. After buying a longer plug-in cord at a local hardware shop, she placed it on her study desk—and now has enough warm light for evening sketching without paying €85 for a new designer lamp.

2. Leo’s Cropped Denim Jacket (€9): Leo found a heavy blue jacket at a charity stall. The sleeves were slightly too long for his arms and two buttons were missing, so he hesitated at first. However, the seller gave him a €2 discount and a handwritten receipt. Later that weekend, Leo trimmed the cuffs, sewed on mismatched vintage buttons, and upcycled it into his favourite everyday jacket.

3. Clara’s Stoneware Mixing Bowl (€7): Clara picked up a hand-thrown ceramic bowl from a crate of kitchenware. The stallholder didn't have enough change for Clara's €20 note, so she bought a €2 iced coffee at the bakery stall next door to get smaller coins. Back home, she realized there were too many knife scratches inside the bowl for mixing bread dough, so she now uses it as a colourful fruit bowl on her kitchen island.

Stage 4 • Grammar Lab: Too vs. Enough 12 MIN • DISCOVERY & 2 DRILLS

Grammar Lab: too vs. enough (+ too much / too many)

Study the patterns from the story, answer 3 quick CCQs, and complete both controlled drills (4A & 4B).

Examples from the Market Story:
• “The power cord was too short to reach her wall socket...” (too + adjective)
• “...now has enough warm light for evening sketching...” (enough + noun)
• “...there were too many knife scratches inside...” (too many + plural countable noun)
TOO = MORE THAN NEEDED / PROBLEM

Using too

  • too + adjective / adverb: too expensive, too tight, too fast
  • too much + uncountable noun: too much clutter, too much cash
  • too many + plural countable noun: too many stains, too many crowds
ENOUGH = SUFFICIENT / RIGHT AMOUNT

Using enough

  • adjective / adverb + enough: warm enough, big enough, early enough
  • enough + noun: enough change, enough pockets, enough time
  • not + adj + enough: The cord wasn't long enough.

You want a pre-loved jacket or lamp, and you have €20 in cash.

  • Ask about the price and check if it fits or works.

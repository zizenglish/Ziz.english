Step 2 • Grammar Focus

Past Simple vs. Present Perfect

Compare when and how we use both tenses.

🕒 Past Simple

Form: V2 / V-ed  |  Negative: didn't + V1  |  Question: Did + S + V1?
Finished time: We lived in Japan from 1995–1998.
Definite time: I saw the Eiffel Tower in 2007.
Series of finished actions: First, he read the book and then he watched the movie.
Time Phrases:
yesterday ... ago last week/year in 1994 when I was...

✨ Present Perfect

Form: have / has + V3 (past participle)  |  Neg: haven't / hasn't + V3
Unfinished time: I've worked as a teacher since 2011.
Indefinite time / Experience: Have you ever seen this movie?
Result: She has already watched this movie 3 times.
Time Phrases:
already just yet ever / never for / since today / this week

📍 Gone vs. Been

Notice the difference between going somewhere and returning!

She's gone to Florida.
She went to Florida and she is still there now.
She's been to Florida.
She went to Florida in the past and has already come back.
